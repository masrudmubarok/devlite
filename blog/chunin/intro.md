# Welcome to Chunin Level

The **Chunin** level is for those who have mastered the basics and are ready to take on more advanced challenges in software development. At this stage, we dive deeper into algorithms and problem-solving.

## What You'll Learn

- **Advanced Algorithms**: Understand and implement more complex algorithms.
- **Object-Oriented Programming**: Learn the concepts of classes, inheritance, and polymorphism.
- **Hands-On Projects**: Work on bigger projects that integrate the concepts learned.

As a Chunin, you’ll be expected to think critically and tackle more complicated tasks, just like a ninja in training.

## Requirements

Ensure you have the skills and knowledge from the **Genin** level. You’ll need to be comfortable with coding basics and algorithms before advancing.

## Ready to Level Up?

Let’s begin the next phase of your training! You are now one step closer to becoming a **Jonin**.
